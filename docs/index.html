Your mail stays online.
Your desktop gets lighter.

Luma Mail is a Gmail desktop client with a custom, animated Qt Quick interface and a Rust engine. Read, compose, reply, search and organize mail directly in your Google account, with configurable limits on disposable local caching.

Download for Linux or WindowsBuild status and release criteria

Current release: 0.11 early access, on the road to 1.0.

Sign in with Google opens your normal browser. Google verification is in progress; warning screens, Google's unverified-user limit and account or Workspace policies may still restrict access. Target Linux desktop acceptance, sustained resource goals and other documented release gates remain open.

Online first

Search runs on Gmail. Conversation lists load in bounded pages; bodies and attachments load on demand. Drafts are saved to Gmail. Optional original-body caching defaults to 100 MiB and can be disabled entirely.

A familiar workspace

Account and unified folders, labels, threading, read/star/importance actions, archive, recoverable Trash, rich composition, inline replies, online signatures and in-memory attachment previews.

Direct to Google

The desktop connects directly to Google APIs. Luma has no hosted mail-processing backend. Refresh tokens use Linux Secret Service or Windows Credential Manager; access tokens stay in memory.

You choose the optional features

Gmail access provides the email-client functionality. Optional read-only Contacts access finds matching saved-contact pictures. Optional basic Gmail settings access manages Luma's sender filters and your primary sending address's online signature. Optional app-limited Drive access uploads selected large attachments and signature pictures.

Optional scopes are offered together at sign-in. Skipping them leaves ordinary mail usable. Settings lets you disable Contact photos and Drive links; Google Account connections lets you revoke Luma's authorization. Disabling a feature does not itself revoke an already-issued Google grant or remove existing server-side filters or shared files. Read the permission-by-permission explanation and controls and privacy policy.

Transparent by design

No advertising, analytics or generalized AI training with Google user data. Received pictures and previews use bounded memory; searches and drafts stay online. Settings, essential account metadata, OS credentials, optional recent-recipient suggestions, runtime libraries and user-selected downloads have separate purposes and retention rules, explained in the privacy policy.
